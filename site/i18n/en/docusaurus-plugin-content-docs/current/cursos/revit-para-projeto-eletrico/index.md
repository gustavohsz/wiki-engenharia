Hands-on course on low-voltage building electrical design in Autodesk Revit, following Brazilian practice (127/220 V and 220/380 V systems, NBR 5410).

**Audience:** people who can already navigate Revit (views, levels, families) and know the basics of electrical installations.

**Software version:** Revit 2022 or later. Screens may differ slightly between versions.
