## Summary

The most widely used insulated conductor in Brazilian building installations, known in the market as "cabo flexível 750 V". It has a single flexible copper conductor with PVC insulation and **no sheath**. Because it has no sheath, it may only be installed inside conduits, trunking, closed channels or other enclosed raceways.

This is a **generic** sheet based on the product standards. Before specifying, check the datasheet of the chosen manufacturer.

### Current-carrying capacity: reference values

Conduit embedded in masonry (reference method B1), ambient temperature 30 °C, no grouping. Values from the ABNT NBR 5410 current-carrying capacity table for PVC-insulated copper conductors.

| Size (mm²) | 2 loaded conductors (A) | 3 loaded conductors (A) |
|---|---|---|
| 1.5 | 17.5 | 15.5 |
| 2.5 | 24 | 21 |
| 4 | 32 | 28 |
| 6 | 41 | 36 |
| 10 | 57 | 50 |

:::warning
These values only apply under these conditions. For a different ambient temperature, more than one circuit in the same conduit or a different installation method, apply the NBR 5410 correction factors. Current-carrying capacity is also just one of the sizing criteria.
:::

## Application

- **Use for:** lighting, outlet and feeder circuits in buildings, always inside conduit or another enclosed raceway.
- **Do not use:** directly buried, on open trays or ladders, or exposed to weather. In those cases use sheathed cables (0.6/1 kV single-core or multicore).
- **Colors:** NBR 5410 reserves light blue for neutral and green-yellow (or green) for the protective conductor. Use other colors for phases.
- **Minimum sizes per NBR 5410:** 1.5 mm² for lighting and 2.5 mm² for outlets and power circuits.
- **Places of public assembly**, such as schools, hospitals and shopping centers: check whether halogen-free cables (low smoke and toxic gas emission) are required instead of PVC.
