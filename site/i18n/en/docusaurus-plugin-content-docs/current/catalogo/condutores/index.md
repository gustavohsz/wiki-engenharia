Wires and cables used in low-voltage installations. The sheets describe each **type** of conductor according to the product standards. Values from a specific manufacturer, when present, are identified on the sheet.

## Sheets

- [450/750 V flexible PVC-insulated cable](./cabo-flexivel-450-750-v-pvc.md): the most common conductor in Brazilian building installations, run in conduit.

## Before specifying

- The conductor size comes from sizing per NBR 5410, not just from the current-carrying capacity table. See the [NBR 5410 summary](../../normas/abnt-nbr-5410.md) and the [voltage drop](../../calculos/queda-de-tensao.md) calculation.
- Check the product certification (Inmetro mark). Low-voltage conductors for fixed installations require mandatory certification in Brazil.
