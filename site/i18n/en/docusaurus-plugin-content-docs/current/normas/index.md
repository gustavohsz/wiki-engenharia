Original summaries of the technical standards that apply to electrical installations, with a topic index and links to the official sources.

:::caution Copyright
Technical standards such as ABNT NBR and IEC are paid, copyrighted documents. This wiki **does not reproduce** their text: it only publishes the identification (number, edition, title), an original summary and a link to the official source. Always check the current edition.
:::

## What each summary includes

- Full identification and status (current or superseded).
- Scope and key points, in our own words.
- Where the standard applies in this wiki's calculations and procedures.
